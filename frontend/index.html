Session rules

  • One shared path. The whole class works through a single roadmap together — not ten separate games.
  • One team per challenge. Each team is responsible for exactly one level. When you finish, the next team takes the following challenge.
  • 100-minute session clock. Time counts down while a team is solving. It pauses when a challenge is completed, until the next team starts.
  • When time hits zero, the game stops immediately. No more flag submits, hints, or progress.
  • Scoring. Each challenge is worth 15 pts. Free hint is free. Paid hint −5 (you get 10 pts).
  • Tools. Use Postman (starter kit) or Burp. Submit flags in this UI.
